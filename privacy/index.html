Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we handle your data.

Overview

Sportsurge is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website or use our application. We believe in transparency and minimal data collection.

Information We Collect

Sportsurge is designed to minimize data collection. We collect:

  • Automatically Collected Data: Standard web server logs including IP address, browser type, referring pages, pages visited, and time spent on the site. This data is used solely for maintaining and improving the service.
  • No Personal Information Required: We do not require registration, login, email, or any personal information to use our service.
  • No Cookies for Tracking: We do not use tracking cookies. Any cookies present are strictly for basic site functionality.

How We Use Your Data

The limited data we collect is used to:

  • Maintain and improve our website and application
  • Monitor site performance and fix technical issues
  • Analyze aggregate traffic patterns to improve user experience
  • Ensure security and prevent abuse

Third-Party Services

Sportsurge may use third-party services such as analytics providers and advertising networks. These services may collect information about your visits to our site and other websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third parties.

When you click on streaming links, you leave our site and are subject to the privacy policies of those third-party streaming sources.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share aggregate, non-personally identifiable information for analytics purposes.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect the limited data we collect. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Sportsurge is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@sportsurge.forum.

Disclaimer
