About Sportsurge

Sportsurge is a free sports streaming guide platform that provides live HD streams for NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, Soccer, UFC, WWE, F1, Cricket, and more across all devices including Android, iOS, PC, Smart TV, and Firestick. Founded in 2021, Sportsurge has grown to serve millions of sports fans worldwide with no registration required.

Our Mission

Sportsurge was created with a simple mission: to provide sports fans with free, easy access to live sports streams without the hassle of subscriptions, registrations, or hidden fees. We believe every fan deserves to watch their favorite teams and athletes compete live, regardless of their budget.

Our platform aggregates streaming links from multiple sources across the web, providing users with 1-5 high-quality streams for every live game or event. We do not host any content ourselves – we simply organize and present available streams in a clean, user-friendly interface.
