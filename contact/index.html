Get in Touch

Whether you have a question about using Sportsurge, want to report an issue, or simply want to share feedback, we're here to help. Reach out to us using any of the methods below.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email:

contact@sportsurge.forum

We aim to respond within 48 hours. For urgent issues regarding broken streams, please include the sport, teams playing, and the time of the game in your message.

What We Can Help With

  • Technical Support – Issues with the website or app not loading properly
  • Stream Issues – Reporting broken or dead stream links
  • Feature Requests – Suggestions for new features or sports to add
  • Feedback – General comments about your experience using Sportsurge
  • Partnerships – Business inquiries and collaboration opportunities
  • Copyright Concerns – If you believe your content is being used improperly

Before You Contact Us

Many common questions can be answered by checking these pages first:

  • App Download Page – How to download and install the app
  • About Sportsurge – Learn what Sportsurge is and how it works
  • About – Frequently asked questions and information
  • Terms of Service – Our terms and conditions
  • Privacy Policy – How we handle your data
  • Disclaimer – Legal disclaimers

Response Time

We are a small team and do our best to respond to all inquiries promptly. Typical response times:

  • General Questions: 24-48 hours
  • Technical Issues: 12-24 hours
  • Broken Streams: 1-6 hours during live events
  • Copyright Concerns: 24-72 hours
